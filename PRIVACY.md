# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-18_

Keepr is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What Keepr does with your data

- **Photos and videos** are read from your device's media library only to display them while you review them.
  They are never uploaded, copied or shared.
- **Deletions** happen only after you confirm them, using Android's system delete flow.
- **Settings and progress** (theme, language, filters, the list of items you chose to keep, statistics and badges)
  are stored locally on your device. Keepr stores media identifiers, not the media itself.
- **Notifications** (optional) are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

## What Keepr does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs
- No network requests with your data — Keepr has no backend
- No location, camera, microphone or contacts access

## Permissions

See the permissions table in the [README](README.md#permissions) for what each Android permission is used for.

## Backups

Android may include the app's local settings in your device or cloud backup, according to your system settings.
Uninstalling Keepr removes its local data.

## Children

Keepr does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are committed to this repository. Questions can be raised through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ader-Land/Keepr/issues).
